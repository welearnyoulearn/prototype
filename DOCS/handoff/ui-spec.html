Billing screens, in the portal you already have

The same plans, usage, billing and traffic work, redrawn to look and behave like today's portal: same layout, fonts, colours, tables and drawers. Fewer, simpler screens: one new menu item, two renamed, and everything else lives inside pages people already use.

How we keep it clean
Reuse, don't addExisting shell, sidebar, tabs, drawer, tables, buttons (components/ui) and portal.css. No new visual style.
One place per jobPrices live with plans. Usage and bills live in Billing. Traffic stays where analytics is today.
Tables, not dashboardsOne metrics strip at most, then a table. Details open in the side drawer the portal already uses.
Show the next stepEvery warning says what happens and what to do. Money actions confirm in the row, restating the amount.
Hide what doesn't applyServices not in a plan aren't shown as 0/0; track-only services have no prices; buttons appear only when usable.

Screens

Eight screens

Shared rules (unchanged from the previous plan)
Money, numbers, months, states, permissions, accessibility
  • Money: ₹ with Indian grouping, 2 decimals on bills, none on plan prices; GST always its own line. One <Money/> component.
  • Big numbers: full below 10,000; short form above ("1.45M tokens") with the exact number in a tooltip.
  • Months: IST calendar months; "October 2026"; ranges "1 – 31 Oct".
  • Status: a word on every chip; green fine, amber attention, red blocked/overdue, grey closed/neutral.
  • Loading: skeletons shaped like the table; keep old data while refetching. Errors: inline with a retry, input kept.
  • Two admins: saves send updated_at; a stale save says who changed it and keeps the input.
  • Permissions: hide what a role can't do; billing is school admin + principal only Q10.
  • Accessibility: labelled inputs, progress bars with text, keyboard reach, focus returns after the drawer closes.
  • Test IDs: {screen}-{element}[-{id}] on every interactive element.
