## What it does

Create classes and connect them to teachers, subjects and students.

## Who uses it

School admin.

## How it works

- Create a class (grade + section) and assign the **class teacher** (who gets the *My class* attendance dashboard).
- Add subjects to a class and assign a teacher to each; assign syllabus.
- Students tab lists the class; click a name to open the student's full profile.
- Overview shows recent attendance for the class.

## Rules and limits

- A class belongs to one school; classes can be soft-deleted.
