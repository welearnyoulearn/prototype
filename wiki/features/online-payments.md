# Feature: Online Fee Payments (UPI)

**Platform Admin feature key:** `online-payments`
**Portal:** School Admin / Parent
**Status:** Built as a manual, verified UPI flow — there is no payment gateway
**Last verified against the code:** 2026-09-21

---

## What it does

A UPI-based way for parents to pay fees — currently a manual, verified flow rather than a payment gateway.

## Who uses it

Parents (pay/report), school admin (verify).

## How it works

- The school sets its UPI id / QR (`/api/fees/upi-id`, `/api/fees/upi-qr`).
- The parent pays with any UPI app and **reports** the payment (`POST /api/parent/fees`).
- The payment appears as pending; the admin **verifies** it (`/api/fees/payments/verify`) and it posts to the ledger.

## Rules and limits

- **There is no payment gateway**: no order creation, no webhook, no automatic confirmation. Gateway tables exist only as scaffolding.
- Payment-gateway secrets would need field-level encryption first (planned).
