## What it does

Plan and track what has been taught: a master syllabus each school can customise, tracked by teachers and visible to students and parents.

## Who uses it

Platform admin (master catalog), school admin (customise, analytics), teachers (mark covered), students and parents (view).

## How it works

- The platform keeps a **master catalog** (subjects → chapters → topics → tasks). A school subscribes and gets its **own copy**, which it can edit.
- Classes get a subject + teacher; teachers mark topics covered with a date; coverage analytics and trend charts for the admin.
- Teachers can type names in English letters and convert them to Telugu/Hindi script.

## Rules and limits

- The school copy is independent — later edits to the master do not change it.
- Student and parent views read the school's copy.
