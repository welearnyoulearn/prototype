## What it does

The school year on one calendar: holidays, exams, events and meetings. A holiday closes attendance.

## Who uses it

School admin edits; teachers, students and parents see it read-only.

## How it works

- Month grid; add, edit, delete entries (a date or a range up to 366 days); audience 'everyone' or 'staff only'.
- **Weekly-off** weekdays (default Sunday).
- Adding a holiday over days that already have attendance asks for confirmation; deleting it makes those records count again.

## Rules and limits

- Holidays and weekly-off days are excluded from every attendance percentage.
- Only the school admin can change the calendar.
