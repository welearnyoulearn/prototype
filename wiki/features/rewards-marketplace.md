# Feature: Rewards Marketplace

**Platform Admin feature key:** `rewards-marketplace`
**Portal:** Student, Parent, School Admin (Platform Admin runs it)
**Status:** Planned. Only the foundation exists (feature key seeded OFF at every tier, database tables, starter catalog, points helper). There are no screens or API routes yet.
**Last updated:** 2026-10-28

---

## What it does

Students and parents spend reward points on simple stationery. WLYL owns the catalog and supplies the items; the school admin hands them to the children. Everything is shown in points, never rupees.

## How it will work

- **Catalog (WLYL-owned, one for the whole platform):** pencil, eraser, sharpener (150 points each), blue pen (250), black pen (400), ruler and glue stick (500). WLYL can edit, reprice or switch off any item. The internal cost per item is visible to platform admins only.
- **Ordering:** a student, or a parent for their child, picks an item. The points are taken off the balance at once (a negative row in the points ledger). Up to 500 points' worth per child per calendar month (IST). A child can cancel until the month's run closes, and the points come back.
- **Monthly delivery run:** orders close at the end of the month. WLYL gets a shopping list (item × quantity, per school and in total) and a parcel list for each school, buys exactly what was ordered and sends one parcel per school.
- **At the school:** the school admin taps "Parcel arrived" (children are told to collect from the school office), then ticks each child "Collected". If a child has left, the admin taps "Can't deliver" and the points are refunded.
- **WLYL is told on every order:** an email with the school, the order and the running item counts for that school ("Pencil ×10, Eraser ×20").
- **Tracking:** the Platform Admin Rewards page shows, per school, items Ordered, Sent, At school, Collected and Waiting (red when waiting too long). These are counts of the orders, with no separate bookkeeping.

## Points are one balance

Quiz, doubt and newspaper points are rows in the same ledger (`student_points`). The balance is the sum of a child's rows; spending is a negative row and a refund a positive one. The marketplace therefore spends points earned anywhere. The quiz leaderboard counts only quiz actions, so spending never changes anyone's rank.
