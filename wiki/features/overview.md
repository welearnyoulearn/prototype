## What it does

The school admin's home screen: the school at a glance the moment they sign in.

## Who uses it

School admin, principal, vice principal.

## How it works

- Counts of teachers, students and classes, and the current academic year.
- Today's attendance summary (Morning marked in N of M classes) — shows a **holiday banner instead** when today is a holiday or weekly off.
- Upcoming exams and fee collection percentage.
- One batched call (`GET /api/admin/overview`) so the page loads fast.

## Rules and limits

- The school always comes from the login; a `school_id` for another school is refused (403).
- Attendance figures use the shared attendance rules (see attendance.md).
