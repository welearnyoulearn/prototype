## What it does

Download the school's data.

## Who uses it

School admin.

## How it works

- Attendance: the class register (date range) and the daily absentee list with parent phone numbers (`/api/export/attendance`).
- Marks exports (`/api/export/marks`) by exam/class.

## Rules and limits

- CSV files open safely in Excel (formula characters neutralised) and read Telugu/Hindi names correctly (UTF-8 with BOM).
- Class and school are checked against the login.
