## What it does

A shared library of learning material, plus each school's own textbook uploads.

## Who uses it

School admin, teachers, students and parents (read); platform admin (master library).

## How it works

- The **platform admin** maintains a master library of materials per subject.
- A school subscribes and its classes see the materials for their subjects (`GET /api/school/library`).
- Teachers/admin upload textbooks (`/api/textbooks`); the text is split into chunks and stored for future search (no AI tutor uses it yet).

## Rules and limits

- Visibility follows the school's plan and the class's subjects.
- Uploads are school-scoped.
