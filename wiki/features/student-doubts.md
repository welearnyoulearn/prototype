# Feature: Student Doubts

**Platform Admin feature key:** `student-doubts`
**Portal:** School Admin / Teacher / Student / Parent
**Status:** Built (#435) on top of the Daily Quiz; needs both `daily-quiz` and `student-doubts` on. Escalation to management comes after the pilot (#436). The feature key is seeded OFF at every tier.
**Last updated:** 2026-10-29

---

## What it does

Right after finishing a daily quiz (#450), a student reads the explanation for each question. Not convinced, or still confused? **Ask your teacher**, right there. A doubt is clarified only when the student **proves** they understood by answering a fresh question on the same topic. Then the student earns reward points and the teacher who explained earns **WLYL Stars**.

## How it works (built, #435)

- **AI help → the AI Tutor (#450):** the built-in "Ask WLYL AI" button is gone. `lib/tutorHandoff.ts` builds the question, the options, the student's answer, the right one, the explanation and an optional "what's confusing" into a first message for the student AI Tutor (AI Hub) and opens it. Since #476 the button shows for students whose school has the AI Pro tutor (no env flag): the tutor chat takes the hand-off when it opens (or when it is already open), starts a **new chat in that subject** and sends the question as the first message, shortened to the tutor's 1,000-character limit if needed (explanation dropped first). Students without the tutor still see Ask your teacher. Earlier WLYL AI replies stay readable.
- **Ask your teacher** (under every reviewed question): the question, the student's answer and an optional note go to the **subject teacher** (`class_subjects`), else the **class teacher**. One doubt per question, 3 a day. The teacher gets a notification.
- **Teacher → Doubts:**
  - **Class misconceptions:** the 3 most-missed questions in their subjects from each class's latest closed quiz, with “N of M got it wrong”, open doubts, and one tap **“Covered in class”**, which answers every open doubt on it in that class.
  - **Doubts by question:** students stuck on the same question are grouped, so one reply answers all of them. Each shows how many school days it has waited, whether the student tried the earlier WLYL AI, and “answered — not understood yet” when the proof failed.
  - **WLYL Stars:** this month and in all.
- **Proof:** once answered, the student taps **“Try a fresh question”** (same topic, unseen first, never the original, no answer key sent). Right + **“I understand now”** = **clarified**: **+5 points** to the student, **+3 Stars** to the teacher who explained, **+2 more** if it was explained within 2 working days. Wrong = still answered, and they can try another or say **“Still not clear”**, which reopens it for the teacher. If the topic has no other question, the student can mark it understood without points or Stars.
- **Ledgers:** `student_points` and `teacher_star_points` (one row per award, unique per action and doubt, never negative), ready for a future rewards marketplace.
- **Badge:** Comeback (5 proven doubts).
- **Parents** see the child's doubts that are not clarified yet in the **Daily Report** (#450). No push about teachers.
- **School Admin → Daily Quiz:** doubts per teacher (open, overdue over 2 school days, answered, clarified this month) and this month's **Star teachers**.

This is a new build. It is **not** the removed Ask a Doubt (#137), and it uses different tables and a different feature key.
